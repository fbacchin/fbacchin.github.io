Euribor X

The European money market in your pocket: €STR and five Euribor tenors — 1 week, 1, 3, 6 and 12 months — with historical charts, a reasoned reading of the curve and a mortgage simulator. On iPhone and iPad.

One card per rate

Each rate is a card: the latest fixing in large type, the change in basis points from the previous one — green if it rises, red if it falls — and a mini chart of the last thirty days. When a rate is stuck at a different date from the others, a badge tells you: that’s normal, the ECB publishes €STR one business day later than Euribor.

Below the cards is the Curve reading: it compares the short end with the longer tenors and tells you, in plain words, whether the market expects hikes, cuts or stability.

The historical chart

Tap a card and the historical series opens: ranges from one month to the full history available, and sliding your finger along the chart reads the exact value for each day.

The mortgage simulator

In the My mortgage tab you set the amount, the term, the reference index — prefilled with the latest fixing — and the bank’s spread: the payment is recalculated instantly. The “What if rates…” slider shows the payment if the index rose or fell by up to two points: how exposed you really are to the ECB’s moves, on one screen.

Offline too

The data updates by itself at launch and stays available offline. Fixings are only published on business days — if today’s is missing, nothing is broken: it arrives tomorrow.

Free, and the ads can go

Since version 7.2 there is a single app, Euribor X: free and complete, supported by ads. And you can remove the ads: with an annual subscription, or a one-time purchase that lasts forever.

The old paid version is now called Euribor Legacy: it keeps working but no longer gets updates. If you bought it, you can use Euribor X without ads and without paying again — the support page explains how.
