Just open them

Three single pages, hosted here: nothing to install, no account. Open the link and the maths is already done.

Euribor today

Today’s fixings for every tenor, the chart for the year and what they mean for the payment on a variable-rate mortgage. It updates by itself.

Euribor Weekly

Every Monday morning, one email: the week’s Euribor rates, how much they moved and what changes for the payment on a variable-rate mortgage. Free. The first issue comes out on Monday 19 October 2026. The newsletter is in Italian.

Your first name, last name and email are used only to send you the newsletter. You’ll get an email to confirm, and you can unsubscribe with one click from any issue. Privacy.

The workshop

The data doesn’t update itself. The Gotthard collector runs on GitHub Actions and samples the state of the tunnel every five minutes; the fixings become public CSVs as soon as they come out, on the same day they are set.

Everything underneath these apps lives in a single repo: github.com/fbacchin/app.
